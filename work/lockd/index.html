Productivity, Health & Fitness

Discipline that does not rely on willpower.

LOCKD is a focus app for people who keep losing momentum to the day they took off. It blocks distracting apps, holds a daily list of non-negotiables, and defends the streak with rules rather than motivation.

The problem

One day off turns into three

Most focus apps treat distraction as a willpower problem. They offer a timer, a quote, and a button you can press to turn the whole thing off. The moment the day gets hard, the app is the first thing to lose.

The failure is not the first missed day. It is what the missed day does to the streak, and what a broken streak does to the habit.

What we built

The parts that mattered.

01

Blocking with no loopholes

App blocking that holds when the user least wants it to, so focus is enforced by the system rather than renegotiated every afternoon.

02

A reason attached to the routine

A goal the user writes themselves sits at the top of the home screen, so the daily list is anchored to something specific rather than abstract.

03

Non-negotiables, tracked daily

A short list of things that have to happen, with completion tracked against the streak so progress is measured by the habit, not by app usage.

04

Streaks that mean something

Because the streak is tied to the routine rather than to opening the app, it reflects real consistency instead of attendance.
