The problem

Nobody reads an ingredient label in a supermarket

The information is already printed on the package. It is just unreadable in practice: eight point type, chemical names most people cannot parse, and a decision that has to be made in about four seconds while holding a basket.

An app only helps here if it is faster than putting the product back on the shelf and moving on.

What we built

The parts that mattered.

01

Barcode scanning built for speed

Camera to verdict in seconds, because anything slower loses to simply not bothering.

02

A grade, not a data dump

Ingredients are assessed against a rulebook and returned as a verdict with the reasoning underneath, so the answer arrives before the detail.

03

Additive and packaging detection

Dyes, preservatives, seed oils and packaging risks surfaced explicitly rather than buried in a list.

04

History and a browsable catalog

Every scan is kept, and everything already graded can be browsed by score, so the app gets more useful the longer it is used.
