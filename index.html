Approach

Most apps die somewhere between the idea and the App Store.

Usually it stalls. A quote comes back from someone cheap, the build drags, and the thing quietly stops being discussed. We exist to close that gap: scope it properly, build it, and get it in front of real users.

The hard part is never the screens

Pure Scan reads a barcode, then grades an ingredient list against a rulebook and returns a verdict fast enough to be useful while you are standing in the aisle. None of that is visible in a screenshot, and it is the first thing a cheap quote quietly leaves out.

That is the work: the logic underneath the interface, shaped around a specific job. The screens are the easy half.

02

03

Questions

The things people ask first.

How long does a build take?

It depends on scope, and we will not pretend otherwise. You get a firm timeline during scoping, before any code is written, plus working builds on your own device throughout so you can see where it stands. More on what actually sets the timeline.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after scoping. There is no rate card, because a two-screen utility and a multi-role platform are not the same job. You get a fixed figure before work starts. Here is what moves the number.

Do I own the app?

Yes. You own the source code. No platform lock-in, no monthly licence keeping your own app alive, and no rebuild required if you ever take it elsewhere.

Do you build for Android too?

Yes. We lead with iOS because that is usually where the users are, and build Android alongside it when your audience calls for it.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes, including the review process. If Apple pushes back, we handle the response rather than sending you the rejection notice. These are the rejections that actually come up.

I only have an idea, not a spec.

That is what scoping is for. Bring the problem and we will work out what version one should be, and what can wait.
