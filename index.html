Approach

Most app builders hand you a template. We start with your problem.

App builders produce something generic, quickly. That works until your business does something specific, and every business worth an app does. We build from scratch, so the software fits the workflow.

The hard part is never the screens

Pure Scan reads a barcode, then grades an ingredient list against a rulebook and returns a verdict fast enough to be useful while you are standing in the aisle. None of that is visible in a screenshot, and none of it comes out of a template.

That is the work: the logic underneath the interface, shaped around a specific job. The screens are the easy half.

Questions

The things people ask first.

How long does a build take?

It depends on scope, and we will not pretend otherwise. You get a firm timeline during scoping, before any code is written, plus working builds on your own device throughout so you can see where it stands.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after scoping. There is no rate card, because a two-screen utility and a multi-role platform are not the same job. You get a fixed figure before work starts.

Do I own the app?

Yes. You own the source code. No platform lock-in, no monthly licence keeping your own app alive, and no rebuild required if you ever take it elsewhere.

Do you build for Android too?

Yes. We lead with iOS because that is usually where the users are, and build Android alongside it when your audience calls for it.

Do you handle App Store submission?

Yes, including the review process. If Apple pushes back, we handle the response rather than sending you the rejection notice.

I only have an idea, not a spec.

That is what scoping is for. Bring the problem and we will work out what version one should be, and what can wait.
