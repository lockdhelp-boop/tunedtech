Approach

Most app builders hand you a template. We start with your problem.

App builders are good at one thing: producing something generic, quickly. That works until your business does something specific — and every business worth building an app for does something specific. We build from scratch, so the software fits the workflow instead of the workflow bending around the software.

01

Built around your workflow

We start by learning how the work actually gets done — the edge cases, the manual steps, the thing everyone works around. That's what the app is designed to solve.

02

Native, not a wrapper

Real native applications that feel like they belong on the device: proper performance, proper gestures, proper offline behavior. Not a website in a shell.

03

Yours to keep

You own what we build. No platform lock-in, no monthly fee holding your app hostage, no rebuild required the day you want to take it elsewhere.

How it works

From first call to the App Store.

01

Scope

We work out what the app needs to do, what it doesn't, and what the first version should be. You get a clear picture of cost and timeline before anything is built.

02

Design

Screens and flows, designed against real use rather than a template's defaults. You see and approve the interface before development starts.

03

Build

Development in visible increments. You get working builds on your own device throughout, so there are no surprises at the end.

04

Launch & maintain

We handle App Store and Google Play submission, including the review process. After launch, we keep it updated as the platforms change.
